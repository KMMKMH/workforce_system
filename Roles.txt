--CEO
View the full organizational dashboard
View employee analytics and performance summaries
View task deadlines through the calendar
Manage high-level accounts such as assigning HR and Accountant roles
View employee records and uploaded CVs
View invoices and payment-related records
Approve bonuses or performance-based rewards
--HR
Create and manage employee accounts
Enter employee information such as ID, full name, age, phone number, work email, password, and CV
Manage attendance records and day-off logging
View employee analytics and attendance statistics
Monitor anomaly flags related to attendance behavior
Manage payroll processing
Access attendance summaries related to salary calculation
View analytics relevant to payment and compensation
Manage invoicing or payment documentation
Record signed payment confirmations after printing and scanning them
--Manager
Monitor team task progress
Monitor team attendance
View task deadlines through the calendar
View team statistics and performance summaries
--Employee
Log into the system
Mark attendance using live face verification
View and update assigned tasks
View personal task deadlines through the calendar
Access personal statistics only
Communicate within allowed chat permissions


Ofcourse, All these roles can chat. Chat goes like this:
1) One on One:
CEO can speak to HR and Accountant and Managers;
Accountant can speak to CEO, HR and Managers;
Employees can only speak to their team Managers and HR;
Managers can speak to everybody;
HR can speak to everybody;
2) Team chat:
Every team has a group chat.
3) Announcements:
CEO, HR and Accountant can post Announcements, where everybody else can just see.
